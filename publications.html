Open-source software & datasets

  • PANGAEA benchmark — open framework for evaluating geospatial foundation models.
  • DEFLECT — parameter-efficient adaptation of GFMs.
  • X-STARS — cross-sensor self-supervised training and alignment.
  • Continual Barlow Twins — continual self-supervised learning.
  • 3D change detection / 3DCD — code and resources for 2D/3D change detection.
  • MARE — self-supervised remote-sensing semantic segmentation.
