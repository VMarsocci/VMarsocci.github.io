Earth observation × machine learning

I study what learned representations really know about the Earth.

I am a Research Fellow at ESA Φ-lab working on geospatial foundation models, representation learning, adaptation and multimodal Earth observation. My research increasingly focuses on understanding when learned Earth representations are transferable, robust and physically meaningful.

Research

From high-performing models to scientific understanding.

I work across machine learning and Earth observation, with a recurring question: what properties of a learned representation remain reliable when sensors, geography, models and downstream tasks change?

01

Geospatial foundation models

Benchmarking, diagnosing and improving general-purpose models for Earth observation across diverse sensors, regions and downstream tasks.

GFMsbenchmarkingtransfer
02

Representation understanding & adaptation

Studying what is encoded in pretrained features, how adaptation rewrites representations and when compact interventions are preferable to full fine-tuning.

PEFTrepresentation geometryadaptation
03

Multimodal & temporal Earth observation

Learning across optical, SAR and other geospatial modalities, with particular interest in robust cross-sensor alignment and change through time.

multimodalSARtime
04

Physical meaning in learned Earth representations

Connecting latent representations to physical state, process directions and observable change rather than treating embeddings as opaque convenience features.

physical groundingobservabilityEarth dynamics
