Research

Research areas

My work combines methodological machine learning with real Earth-observation problems. A recurring theme is understanding not only how well models work, but why they work, what information they encode, and under which conditions their representations remain useful.

  • Geospatial foundation models: design, benchmarking and evaluation.
  • Adaptation strategies for pretrained models and learned representations.
  • Cross-sensor and multimodal self-supervised learning for Earth observation.
  • Temporal learning, continual learning and physical change detection.
  • Scientific interpretation of pixel-level Earth embeddings and their relation to physical/geographical information.
